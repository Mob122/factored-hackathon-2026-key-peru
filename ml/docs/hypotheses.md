## Day 1 decision (2026-09-28)

The proposal's section 4 rule gives **Track B with declared limitations**: A fails (2 of 5 root causes at >= 1%, none above noise) and B fails (B1). B2 cannot change the outcome. B is reframed: no failure-demand thesis, no SLA or Regulator classifiers; the data justification is B5 plus stale complaint statuses (B1), and the evaluated learned component is B6.
