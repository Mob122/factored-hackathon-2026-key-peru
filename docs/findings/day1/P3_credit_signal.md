# P3 - Credit risk signal (days_past_due)

Generated by `ml/analysis/day1/P3_credit_signal.py` on 2026-09-28 over `ml/data/02_intermediate/` (400,000 products, 150,000 customers).

## Question

Is there learnable signal for a credit risk estimate? Target: `days_past_due > 0` and `> 30` on personal loans (`Préstamo Personal`) and credit cards (`Tarjeta Crédito`), using only features known at application: credit_score, estimated_monthly_income, segment, occupation, education_level, country and age at opening. Report the AUC of credit_score alone and of a logistic regression with a temporal split by opening_date, each against a shuffled-label baseline; and outcome distributions by gender, country and segment.

## Verdict: **refuted: no learnable signal; credit_score and the application features rank delinquency at chance level**

- **credit_score alone:** test AUC 0.5016 (dpd>0) and 0.5022 (dpd>30); full-cohort AUC 0.5034 / 0.5036. The permuted-label 95% band is 0.5 ± 0.0113.
- **Logistic regression (temporal split):** test AUC 0.4972 [0.4871, 0.5074] for dpd>0 and 0.5048 [0.4921, 0.5151] for dpd>30, vs 0.5096 / 0.5060 for the same model refit on shuffled labels (95th pct of 30). Train AUC 0.5215 / 0.5219. Test log loss 0.32308 vs 0.32297 for a constant (dpd>30).
- **Best AUC of any model/target: 0.5048**, against a pre-declared materiality bar of 0.55. Learnable signal: dpd>0 no, dpd>30 no.
- **Delinquency looks assigned independently of the customer:** the dpd>0 rate is ~14.95% and dpd>30 ~9.98% in every credit_score decile, opening year, product type and group (sections d-e).
- **Consequence:** a credit risk score cannot be built from this dataset; any model would output the base rate. Do not ship or demo a delinquency / risk estimate as a feature, and do not present credit_score as a risk driver. If a risk field is needed in the UI, show credit_score as a raw attribute only.

## (b) Setup

- Temporal split: train = opening_date < 2024-11-09 (91,196 products), test = opening_date >= 2024-11-09 (22,830, latest 20.00%). 12,558 test products belong to customers with no product in train.
- Target rates: dpd>0 14.91% train / 15.11% test; dpd>30 10.00% / 9.90%.
- credit_score alone: score = -credit_score (higher score, lower risk), no fitting; rows with null credit_score excluded.
- Logistic regression: L2 (lambda 1.0), 37 features: credit_score, income_z, age and age², missing flags for each, one-hot segment, occupation (null as a level), education_level (null as a level), country and product_type. Numeric features median-imputed and standardized on train.
- Baselines: 1000 permutations of the test labels against fixed scores, and 30 refits of the logistic regression on shuffled train labels. 95% CIs of the test AUC from 200 bootstrap resamples.

## Method notes

- AUC is computed from average ranks (ties share the mean rank). The credit_score null is two-sided because the expected direction was not assumed.
- sklearn and scipy are not in the environment; the logistic regression is a Newton / IRLS fit in numpy.
- The unit is the product; customers with several credit products appear more than once. The test AUC on customers unseen in train is also at chance, so the result does not depend on repeated customers.
