# A1 - Unrecognized charges as a share of demand

Generated by `ml/analysis/day1/A1_unrecognized_charges.py` on 2026-09-28 over `ml/data/02_intermediate/` (67,095 complaints, 686,296 interactions, 171,321 transcripts). Country comes from `customers` via `customer_id`; year is the calendar year of `creation_date` (2023 starts on 2023-06-17 and 2026 ends on 2026-06-18, so both are partial).

## Hypothesis

Unrecognized charges (and related disputes, fraud and chargebacks) are a relevant share of customer-service demand. If not, Track A is dropped.

## Verdict: **not supported as a differentiating theme**

- **Measurable only in complaints.** No `contact_reason`, transcript intent, topic, keyword or text refers to charges, disputes or fraud, so the theme cannot be seen in the 686,296 interactions or 171,321 transcripts.
- **Fair share, not a standout.** Core unrecognized charges are 20.24% of complaints (rank 1 of 5, with all categories within 0.58 pp of each other) but 1.80% of all contacts (rank 7 of 11). With Fees added: 40.44% and 3.60%.
- **No structure.** The category mix does not depend on country (p = 0.19), reception channel (p = 0.75) or year (p = 0.72), and core outcomes look like the rest (group vs status p = 0.19).
- **Consequence (from 'Si falla'):** the data give no reason to prioritise unrecognized charges over any other complaint category. Track A can only rest on industry evidence, not on this dataset's demand.
