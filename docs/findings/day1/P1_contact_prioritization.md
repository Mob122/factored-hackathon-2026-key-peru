# P1 - Contact reason prioritization

Generated by `ml/analysis/day1/P1_contact_prioritization.py` on 2026-09-28 over `ml/data/02_intermediate/` (686,296 interactions, 212,759 surveys). Interactions span 2023-06-17 to 2026-06-18.

## Question

Which contact reasons should an AI-first service layer target first? For each `contact_reason` and reason group: volume, handle time, wait, FCR, escalation, follow-up, sentiment and survey scores, and whether any difference between reasons is real or generator noise (shuffled-reason baseline).

## Verdict: **reason differences are real, set by 3 generator knobs (FCR, handle time, sentiment mix); CSAT and follow-up follow from FCR alone; wait and escalation are flat**

- **Real, material differences (beyond the shuffled baseline):** mean duration (s), FCR (was_resolved), requires_followup, mean sentiment_score, negative sentiment share, CSAT mean (1-4), CSAT satisfied (3-4), CES mean (1-4), NPS-survey mean (2-7). FCR runs from 91.51% (Transaccional) to 43.60% (Queja), a 47.91% range against a shuffled 95th percentile of 0.74%. Mean duration runs 220.8-539.9 s (shuffled range 2.9 s).
- **Noise:** mean wait (s), escalation rate, survey coverage. Wait is ~120 s and escalation ~9.96% for every reason. Equal survey coverage means the CSAT comparison is not biased by who gets surveyed.
- **CSAT and follow-up are FCR in disguise.** CSAT is 2.00 when unresolved and 3.00 when resolved, in every reason; `requires_followup` is 100.00% when unresolved and 14.98% when resolved. With `was_resolved` held fixed, the reason gap in CSAT, CES, NPS-survey score and follow-up is at noise level (section g). The reason drives FCR, and FCR drives the rest.
- **Sentiment is a separate knob, and an odd one.** Transaccional is 100.00% `Neutral`, Producto mostly Neutral, and the other 4 reasons share one mix (~35% negative). Within those 4 reasons, CSAT barely moves with sentiment (section g), so sentiment is not a usable satisfaction proxy.
- **Priority depends on the lever.** For deflection by volume: Transaccional (34.98% of contacts, 91.51% FCR, shortest calls). For failure reduction: Queja (17.05% of contacts but 41.18% of unresolved contacts and 23.08% of handle time, CSAT 3-4 45.47%). Técnico is second on unresolved contacts (19.31%).
- **Grouping caveat.** The source has 6 coarse labels. Card servicing and account inquiry have no source label; `Producto` is mapped to credit product as the closest fit, but it may also contain card and account questions. `other` pools Técnico, Comercial and Retención, whose outcomes are close but not identical (FCR 60-70%).
- **Consequence:** the reason label is a valid prioritization key. Target Transaccional for automated resolution and Queja for resolution-rate improvement. By construction of the generator, any FCR gain moves CSAT by about 1 point per extra resolved contact, so CSAT uplift is not independent evidence. Escalation and wait time cannot differentiate reasons.
