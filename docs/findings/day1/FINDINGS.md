# Day 1 findings

- B4 (complaints <-> interactions): refuted: no declared link, and the fallback join matches at chance level. origin_interaction_id 0.00% non-null; fallback customer+[-7d,0] matches 2.87% vs 2.73% placebo (lift 1.05x); see docs/findings/day1/B4.md
- C1 (data quality): refuted as stated. 0 duplicates (declared ~2%); 0 late rows (process_date is a 06:00/08:00 business-day cutoff); 2 drifting columns; random nulls at round rates (5-20%), the rest structural; 2/24 FKs with orphans (customers.registration_branch_id, service_agents.assigned_branch_id ~100% broken); complaints.origin_interaction_id 100% null; Spanish labels, no MXN. See docs/findings/day1/C1.md
- A1 (unrecognized charges as demand): not supported as a differentiating theme. Only measurable in complaints: core 20.24% of complaints (rank 1/5, uniform categories) and 1.80% of all contacts; no theme in interactions or transcripts; no structure by country/channel/year; see docs/findings/day1/A1.md
- B3 (Regulator and SLA): volume confirmed, structure refuted: Regulator and SLA-breach volume exist, but neither depends on country, category or customer history. Regulator 717 (1.07%), breached 20.11%; 90d prior complaint/contact 31.07% Regulator vs 33.82% other vs 33.64% placebo; later Regulator after breach 0.03% vs 0.03%; sla_breached independent of resolution_days; see docs/findings/day1/B3.md
- A2 (disputes closed as valid charge): not testable with this dataset. `resolution` is 5 generic texts independent of category and of compensation; 0 say 'valid charge', 0 say fraud; best proxy ('explanation' text, no compensation) 12.88% of decided core disputes; status, compensation and resolution_days match other categories; see docs/findings/day1/A2.md
- A3 (preauth / foreign currency / double charges): refuted as a data-backed pattern. 1,083,406 card purchases; (1) preauthorization 0.00% (base 0.00%); (2) foreign currency 55.05% (base 88.29%); (3a) exact pipeline duplicate 0.00% (base 0.00%); (3b) repeat charge, distinct transaction_id, 10 min 0.00% (base 0.00%); (4) habitual merchant 1.41% (base 1.41%); >= 1.00%: (2), (4); above baseline: none; see docs/findings/day1/A3.md
- A5 (fraud_score leakage): refuted: fraud_score leaks is_fraud by construction. is_fraud 0.10% overall, drifting slightly down by year (p 0.021, holdout 0.09%); fraud_score ROC AUC 0.8469 / PR AUC 0.6946 (holdout 0.8505 / 0.7153), under the 0.95 flag only because the leak is partial: non-fraud scores stop at 30, fraud ~ U(0, 100), 69.28% of frauds above 30 at 100% precision; no post-outcome leakage (status p 0.57, Declined/Reversed RR 0.97); see docs/findings/day1/A5.md
- B1 (failure demand): refuted as material: no excess overall; the only real signal is a small bump (1.17x, about 1.1 extra contacts per 100 complaints) in complaints that actually got resolved or closed, fading within two weeks. Matched equal-length placebo 22,648 vs 22,297 contacts, ratio 1.016 [0.996, 1.034] (32,231 complaints); per customer-day 1.008 (month MH); reason mix p 0.4912, requires_followup 34.40% vs 34.94%; excess slope on resolution_days +0.0003/day (SE 0.0004); see docs/findings/day1/B1.md
- A4 (recurrence and sequence structure): refuted: no recurrence and no customer-level sequence structure. Monthly peak ratio 1.03 (shuffled 0.97); monthly pairs 0.07% vs 0.07% shuffled; monthly series 0.000%; next category (a) customer mode 21.09% vs (b) global 24.91% (-3.83 pp, log-loss gain -5.00%); see docs/findings/day1/A4.md
- P1 (contact reason prioritization): reason differences are real, set by 3 generator knobs (FCR, handle time, sentiment mix); CSAT and follow-up follow from FCR alone; wait and escalation are flat. Transaccional 34.98% of 686,296 contacts at 91.51% FCR; Queja 17.05% of contacts but 41.18% of unresolved; FCR range 47.91% vs shuffled 0.74%; escalation range 0.24% (p 0.5968); card servicing and account inquiry have no source label; see docs/findings/day1/P1_contact_prioritization.md
- P2 (card support workflow): partially supported: cards, their status and per-card transactions exist, but decline codes are random, blocked/suspended cards have no history, and card activity is too sparse for transaction lookup to be hard. 140,040 cards, 5.03% Blocked / 2.04% Suspended, none of them with transactions; 39.53% of holders have 2+ cards; 4 decline codes uniform across statuses (p 0.723); 24 merchants; median 0.0 card tx per 30 days; 0.94% of tx have a same-week same-merchant or similar-amount twin; see docs/findings/day1/P2_card_support.md
- P3 (credit risk signal): refuted: no learnable signal; credit_score and the application features rank delinquency at chance level. 114,026 loans/cards, dpd>0 14.95%, dpd>30 9.98%; test AUC credit_score 0.5016 / 0.5022, logistic regression 0.4972 / 0.5048 vs shuffled 95th pct 0.5096 / 0.5060; gender dpd>30 range 0.20% vs shuffled 0.53%; country dpd>30 range 0.29% vs shuffled 0.56%; segment dpd>30 range 0.35% vs shuffled 1.00%; see docs/findings/day1/P3_credit_signal.md
